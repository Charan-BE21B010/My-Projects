Enterprise Site Safety Handbook - Metro Package 3

Hard hats and high-visibility vests are mandatory for all workers entering Zone A after 08:00.
Scaffolding inspection must be completed every Monday before work begins.
Only certified crane operators may operate tower crane TC-12.
Emergency assembly point is Gate 4 near the material yard.
Maximum wind speed for crane operations is 40 km/h.
Fire extinguishers must be placed every 30 meters along the south corridor.
All near-miss incidents must be logged in the safety portal within 2 hours.
PPE non-compliance fine is INR 2,000 per occurrence.
Night shift work requires additional lighting of at least 100 lux on walkways.
First-aid station is located in Container B next to the site office.
